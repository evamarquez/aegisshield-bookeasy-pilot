# Stakeholder Register and Communication Plan

## Stakeholder Register

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest | Influence | Main concern | Input or decision needed | Engagement approach |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CTO / sponsor | Executive sponsor | Business outcome and delivery risk | High | Scope, schedule, production risk | Approves charter, changes, and go/no-go | Weekly status and decision reviews |
| Project Manager | Coordinates delivery | Progress, risks, dependencies, and decisions | High | Clear ownership and timely escalation | Maintains plan and decisions | Working sessions and written updates |
| Product | Product priorities | Customer value and scope | High | Customer impact and tradeoffs | Confirms priorities and acceptance | Scope and acceptance reviews |
| Engineering | Technical implementation | Supportability and delivery feasibility | High | Compatibility and team capacity | Owns technical design and estimates | Twice-weekly working sessions |
| Security | Security controls | Risk reduction and evidence | Medium | Access and security regressions | Reviews design and test evidence | Design and release checkpoints |
| DevOps / SRE | Environments and operations | Availability and recoverability | High | Release and rollback risk | Confirms monitoring and rollback readiness | Operational readiness reviews |
| QA | Test and validation | Reliable acceptance evidence | Medium | Coverage and unresolved defects | Owns test execution and defect validation | Test planning and defect reviews |
| Customer Success | Customer readiness | Pilot experience and continuity | Medium | Communication and support handoff | Coordinates customer readiness | Weekly pilot preparation check-ins |
| BookEasy | Pilot customer | Working event flow and clear support | Medium | Disruption or unclear expectations | Validates agreed pilot scenarios | Weekly pilot check-ins and acceptance review |
| Support | Post-release support | Clear support path | Medium | Missing runbook or ownership | Reviews handover and known issues | Handover review |
| Compliance / Legal | Obligations and evidence | Contractual and regulatory risk | Medium | Audit and data obligations | Advises when requirements affect scope | As-needed review at decision gates |

## Communication Plan

| Audience | Information needed | Owner | Channel | Frequency | Artifact | Escalation path |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sponsor | Status, risks, decisions, schedule changes | PM | Written status and review | Weekly and at gates | Status report and decision log | PM to CTO |
| Delivery team | Progress, blockers, dependencies, next actions | PM | Working meeting | Twice weekly | Action list and board snapshot | PM to functional lead |
| Product and Customer Success | Scope, customer impact, pilot readiness | PM / Product | Planning and pilot meeting | Weekly | Scope and acceptance notes | Product to sponsor |
| Security, QA, DevOps | Design, test evidence, release readiness | Technical owners / PM | Review meeting | At agreed gates | Test and readiness checklist | Owner to PM, PM to sponsor |
| BookEasy | Pilot timing, scenarios, results, support path | Customer Success | Video call and email | Weekly during pilot | Pilot plan and acceptance record | Customer Success to PM |
| Support | Known issues, runbook, ownership transition | DevOps / PM | Handover review | Before release and at closure | Handover notes and runbook | Support lead to PM |

## Escalation rule

The PM communicates material scope, schedule, customer, security, data, or service-continuity risks as soon as they are known. The sponsor decides when the issue changes scope, release timing, or production approval.
