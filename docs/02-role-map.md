# Role Map and Learning Notes

## Why multiple roles appear in one case

Real teams often separate Business Analyst, Product Manager, and Project Manager responsibilities. In a small company, one person may cover more than one role. For this simulated portfolio case, the same person performs each perspective so the decision process is visible.

The portfolio should still distinguish the responsibilities clearly.

## Responsibility map

| Activity | Business Analyst | Product Manager | Technical Project Manager |
|---|---|---|---|
| Define the business problem | Leads | Contributes | Contributes |
| Investigate current state | Leads | Contributes | Coordinates |
| Identify customer and business impact | Leads | Leads | Contributes |
| Define product priorities | Contributes | Leads | Informs feasibility |
| Build delivery plan | Contributes | Approves priorities | Leads |
| Manage dependencies and risks | Identifies | Helps prioritize | Leads and tracks |
| Coordinate execution | Supports | Participates | Leads |
| Define acceptance criteria | Contributes | Owns product outcome | Coordinates agreement |
| Coordinate release readiness | Contributes | Approves product readiness | Leads operational readiness |

## Stakeholders for the BookEasy pilot

| Stakeholder | What they need | How I would work with them |
|---|---|---|
| Executive sponsor | Clear progress, risks, and decisions needed | Weekly status update and go/no-go decisions |
| Engineering | A realistic plan, clear owners, and protected focus time | Working sessions, dependency tracking, and quick issue escalation |
| Security | Evidence that controls and access rules still work | Review checkpoints before testing and release |
| QA | Test scope, acceptance criteria, and stable environments | Confirm test plan and track defects to closure |
| Product | Customer impact and tradeoffs | Align scope, priorities, and acceptance criteria |
| Customer Success | Customer readiness and a clear handoff | Share pilot plan, training needs, and support notes |
| BookEasy pilot team | Simple expectations, timing, and support path | Regular check-ins, demo, acceptance review, and go-live support |

## My role in this project

As the Junior Technical Project Manager, I would keep the work moving and make sure the right people have the right information. I would not make every technical decision myself. I would bring the team together, track risks and dependencies, document decisions, protect the pilot scope, and make sure release readiness is based on evidence.

## Primary portfolio message

The strongest positioning for this case is:

> I can understand a technical and business problem, help turn it into a prioritized plan, and coordinate the teams needed to deliver it safely.

The emphasis remains Technical Project Manager. The Business Analyst and Product Manager perspectives explain how the project was selected and prioritized.
