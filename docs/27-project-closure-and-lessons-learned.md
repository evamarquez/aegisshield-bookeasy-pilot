## Lessons learned

| Observation | Improvement | Who applies it |
|---|---|---|
| Timeout behavior revealed a duplicate-event defect late. | Test timeouts and stable IDs early. | Engineering and QA |
| Migration required recovery and reconciliation decisions. | Rehearse recovery before go/no-go. | Database, DevOps, Security |
| Customer validation depended on sample events and availability. | Schedule customer review early. | Customer Success and Product |
| Visible decisions and evidence helped recovery. | Repeat status, decision-log, and evidence-gate practices. | PM and functional leads |
