## Support and escalation

Support coverage is business hours, Monday-Friday, Eastern Time, with the on-call DevOps/SRE contact for critical service or ingestion failures outside those hours.

Escalation path: Support lead -> DevOps/SRE -> Engineering lead -> CTO / sponsor for a release, security, data-integrity, or service-continuity decision.

## Acceptance checklist

- Receiving owners have access to the service, monitoring, logs, runbook, and recovery procedures.
- Support reviewed how to monitor service health.
- Support reviewed restore, reconciliation, and application rollback.
- Operations accepted the transfer of support responsibility.

## Key terms

**Handover:** Transfer of service, documents, and support ownership.  
**Runbook:** Step-by-step operating and recovery instructions.  
**Escalation:** Moving an issue to a role with more authority or technical ownership.  
**Known limitations:** Conditions the service does not cover or that remain outside project scope.
